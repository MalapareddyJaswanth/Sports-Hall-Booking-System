# Scalability & Future Plan

Our roadmap for scaling the Sports Hall Booking System:

## 1. Calendar Integration (API)
*   **Concept**: Integrate with Google Calendar API and Microsoft Graph API.
*   **Value**: Sync booking confirmations directly to students' personal calendars.

## 2. Automated SMS Alerts
*   **Concept**: Integrate with Twilio for instant text messages.
*   **Value**: Real-time notifications for approval/rejection without requiring students to check emails.

## 3. QR-Code Gate Access
*   **Concept**: Generate a unique QR code on booking approval.
*   **Value**: Students scan the QR code at the hall gate to open doors, automatically marking the booking as "In Progress" and updating slot status.
