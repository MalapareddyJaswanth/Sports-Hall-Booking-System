# Communication

This document outlines team communication, stand-up schedules, and tooling.

## Collaboration Channels:
*   **Daily Stand-ups**: Held virtually via Google Meet at 10:00 AM (Monday - Friday).
*   **Task Board**: Trello Board used for story progression and assignment tracking.
*   **Code Review**: GitHub Pull Request comments and reviews.
*   **Group Chat**: WhatsApp team channel for instant syncups and quick block resolutions.

## Stand-up Focus:
1.  What did I work on yesterday?
2.  What will I work on today?
3.  What are the blockers?
