# Sample Project Documentation (User Guide)

This document serves as the User Manual for students and coordinators.

## 1. Student User Guide
1.  Navigate to the **Service Portal homepage**.
2.  Click on the **Sports Hall Booking** link.
3.  **Step 1: Select Facility**: Click on a sports hall card (e.g. Badminton Court).
4.  **Step 2: Choose Date & Slot**: Select the date in the picker, then click on any available **Green** time slot.
5.  **Step 3: Reservation Details**: Add remarks/purpose and click **Confirm Reservation**.
6.  **Dashboard**: Track your requests at the bottom of the page. You can click **Cancel** on pending or approved entries.

---

## 2. Facility Coordinator Guide
1.  Log in to your ServiceNow instance.
2.  Open **My Approvals** on the dashboard.
3.  Review pending requests and click **Approve** or **Reject**.
4.  The system will automatically notify the student and reject competing requests.
